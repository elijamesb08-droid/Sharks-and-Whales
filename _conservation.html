Our Oceans Are Calling

Guardians of the Deep

Sharks and whales are the keystones of marine health. Today, they face unprecedented threats from our world above. We must act now to ensure their silence isn't permanent.

Critical Threats

The delicate balance of the deep sea is being disrupted by three primary human-driven forces. Understanding these threats is the first step toward mitigation.

Industrial Overfishing

Each year, millions of sharks are caught as bycatch. Whale migration paths are increasingly obstructed by ghost nets and deep-sea trawling equipment.

Chemical & Noise Pollution

From microplastics found in shark stomachs to sonar testing that deafens whales, the invisible threats are often the most lethal. We are mapping these acoustic impacts in real-time.
