Megalodon: The Ancient Ruler

Dominating the oceans for over 13 million years, Otodus megalodon was the ultimate apex predator to ever swim the Earth's waters.

Hunting Prowess

As a macro-predator, the Megalodon's diet consisted primarily of ancient cetaceans. Their hunting strategy involved immobilizing prey by biting off fins or tail flukes before delivering a crushing blow to the torso.

From Myth to Conservation

The Megalodon may be extinct, but its legacy lives on in the Great White. Protecting today's apex predators is critical for maintaining the health of our global oceans. Without them, the delicate balance of the marine ecosystem collapses.
